## The problem

A major healthcare enterprise customer needed departments to self-serve their own content and templates, while central teams kept control of branding and compliance.

## My contribution

I owned authentication (OAuth, SAML, SCIM), user management and resource management for products serving millions of users. I led a fine-grained access management foundation built together with the customer, coordinating three product teams and leading two of them.

## The outcome

Spaces became a new revenue stream through vertical use cases, tracked with KPIs for upsell, new sales and usage.
