## The problem

Accessibility was treated as a late-stage fix, and the design system and frontend were not built to support it.

## My contribution

I owned the roadmap and strategy for accessibility and the design system. I ran the accessibility program across strategy, audits, reporting, user research, go-to-market and training, and I founded the design system team.

## The outcome

Product, engineering and sales moved toward WCAG-by-default. The work supported multi-million-dollar renewals. Related frontend foundations work cut build times by 75%+ and flaky tests by 80%, across 5+ teams and 20+ systems.
