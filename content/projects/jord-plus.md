## The problem

Construction generates huge amounts of waste because soil data is scattered across public and private sources.

## My contribution

I'm co-founding a digital soil-management platform. I use AI across the whole product lifecycle: research, prototyping, design review and backlog management. I validate AI features with customers.

## Where it stands

It's early. The work so far shows how AI changes workflows and creates new challenges. One principle comes straight from customer research: every AI answer must trace back to a validated source.
