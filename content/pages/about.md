I'm Elena Nazzi, founder of Neon Purple Studio. I help companies with product, accessibility and team collaboration.

I have 20+ years across engineering, research, design and product. I started as a researcher and frontend developer. At Templafy, an enterprise SaaS company, I worked as product owner, engineering manager, UX lead and product manager from 2015 to 2025.

I'm a Certified Professional in Accessibility Core Competencies (CPACC). I teach UX and coding as a volunteer. I'm co-founding JORD+, a soil-management platform built with and for AI.

I think in systems: how all the pieces work together in the product for the user, and in the teams for the organization.
